CASE STUDY / AGRITECH

OBA AgroTech.
A digital system for modern poultry work.

A mobile-first agricultural platform designed to communicate expertise, tools and practical farm support in one premium experience.

THE CHALLENGE

Turn practical agricultural knowledge into a digital experience.

The project needed to present poultry-health knowledge, digital tools and support services clearly without feeling like a generic agricultural website. The direction focused on trust, usefulness, strong information hierarchy and mobile usability.

Focus: positioning, interface design, responsive development and conversion pathways.

01

Position

Make the brand feel modern, credible and useful to poultry farmers.

02

Educate

Organize complex farm information into accessible digital resources.

03

Convert

Create clear routes into tools, products and expert conversations.
