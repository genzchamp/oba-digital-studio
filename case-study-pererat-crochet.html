DEMO CASE STUDY / CROCHET

Pererat Crochet.
A warm digital showcase for handmade brands.

This is a website demo for crochet artists, handmade businesses and creative product brands that want to present their work beautifully and make it easier for customers to enquire.

THE PROBLEM

Handmade products deserve a digital experience that communicates their care and personality.

The concept gives a handmade brand space to display its products, explain its style and guide interested visitors toward conversations or orders.

Focus: visual storytelling, product presentation, brand warmth and mobile-first browsing.

01

Show the craft

Present handmade work with visual space and clear descriptions.

02

Communicate style

Use layout and tone to make the creative identity feel consistent.

03

Invite enquiries

Help visitors move naturally from discovery to customer conversation.
